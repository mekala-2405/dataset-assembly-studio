BLOCKING VALIDATION

Preflight

Nothing is silently dropped. Resolve every blocker before export.

What it verifies:

  • Every approved checkpoint is present and readable.
  • The two-camera output contract holds across the whole selection.
  • Joint and camera mappings are consistent with the export schema.
  • Task-group caps are respected and no checkpoint is dropped silently.
